A bio-inspired, odometry-free neuromorphic framework for fully onboard, vision-only UAV navigation and dynamic obstacle avoidance in unknown, signal-denied environments. It achieves 2.54 ms latency, avoids obstacles moving at up to 15 m/s, and uses 20.38% of the energy consumed by a traditional architecture.
